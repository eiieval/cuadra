Generated from the issuer's ledger copy. For legal effect, scan the AEAT QR. Nothing is sent to any server: the record travels in the part of the link after the #, which browsers keep to themselves.

What does this check?

This page rebuilds the record from the link and recomputes its SHA-256 hash with the field order of the AEAT specification: issuer NIF, invoice number, date, invoice type, VAT total, total, hash of the previous record and generation time. It also recomputes the totals from the invoice lines and compares the link to the previous record. Client names and descriptions are not part of the VeriFactu hash, and the page cannot know whether the invoice was cancelled later: the AEAT QR code is what the tax agency checks.
