1 · Ask
Describe the sale in plain English or Spanish. The agent drafts the invoice, reuses known clients and checks NIF and VAT. Nothing happens until you confirm.
2 · Comply
Every invoice and every cancellation is a VeriFactu record: SHA-256 hash chained to the previous one, AEAT verification QR and XML. Records are never edited, only appended.
3 · Collect
PayPal Invoicing sends it with its tax-agency link and collects. The agent chases late payers, records transfers and drafts your quarterly VAT.
